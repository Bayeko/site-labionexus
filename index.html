The field

Lab automation and lab data, in regulated environments.

Laboratories automate: robots, instrument software, interfaces into the laboratory information system. In a regulated environment, each of those pieces is qualified before use, each change is assessed, and the data they produce has to stand in a record that someone is accountable for.

Labionexus works in that space: the automation that produces a result, and the data path that carries it into the record that has to trust it.
